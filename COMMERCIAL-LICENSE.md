- **Commercial use** of every `@talosjs/*` package, the `talos` CLI and the Skeleton project template.
- **Unlimited developers** within the licensed legal entity.
- **Unlimited projects and deployments**: internal tools, SaaS products, client work, on-premise or cloud.

  - Modified versions must not be published, shared, sold, sublicensed or distributed in source form to anyone outside the licensed legal entity, including clients, partners, subsidiaries and affiliates.

  - Modifications must not remove, bypass or disable license checks, attribution or any other licensing mechanism.
  - Modifications remain subject to this license and stop being usable if the license ends, except on the versions covered by the perpetual fallback.
  - Ooneex keeps all rights to Talos. Any fix or improvement you send back to Ooneex can be included in Talos without compensation.

- **Perpetual fallback**: every version released while your license is active stays yours to use forever, even if you don't renew.

### 2. Framework and tooling

- All 62 packages: HTTP and routing, data and persistence, auth and permissions, real-time, queues, cron, workflow, AI, payments, mailer, storage, analytics and more.
- The `talos` CLI (alias `oo`): app, module and microservice scaffolding, code generators (controllers, entities, services, repositories, migrations, seeds, middleware, crons, events…), `check`, `fmt`, `lint`, `test`, `coverage`, `build`, `release:create`, Docker and npm publishing.
- **Quality and security tooling**: `talos security:check` (OSV.dev audit of every lockfile plus LLM config audit) and `talos performance:check`.
- **AI assistant integration**: `talos agent:skills:create` installs the curated skills, agents and project rules for the eleven supported coding assistants (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and others), kept in sync with the framework.
- Shell completions for zsh, bash and fish.

### 3. Updates

- **Security patches** with priority, including backports to the previous major version for 12 months after a new major ships.
- **Upgrade guides** and changelogs for every breaking change, with CLI-assisted migrations (`talos update`) where possible.

### 6. Roadmap

## Renewal and cancellation

- The license renews monthly. You can cancel at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month, with no refund for the month already started.
- Without renewal you keep the right to use every version released during your active term, but you stop receiving updates, security patches and support.
- Renewing after a lapse re-opens access to the latest version from the renewal date.
